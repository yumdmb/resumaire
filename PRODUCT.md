# Resumaire

## Product Purpose

A job application tracker with structured resume editing and honest AI-assisted tailoring. Helps job seekers manage active applications, maintain a base resume, and generate tailored versions grounded in their real experience.

## Users

Early-career job seekers actively working through 5–20 applications at a time. They're organized, methodical, and applying across multiple roles. They need clarity, not hand-holding.

## Brand Tone

Calm and trustworthy. The tool should feel like a well-designed notebook: quiet, structured, reliable. It disappears into the task.

## Register

product

## Design Anchors

Linear, Raycast.

## Anti-References

None specified.

## Strategic Principles

- Every word earns its place. No filler copy, no AI-sounding explanations.
- The interface serves the task. Users are in flow; don't interrupt it.
- Honest by design. AI suggestions are proposals, not facts. User review is mandatory.
- Structured over freeform. Resume sections are typed and editable, not a text blob.
